## Overview

Categories help you organize and classify your transactions. They make it easy to understand where your money goes.

::app-media
---
name: categories
---
::

## Hierarchical Structure

Finapp supports parent-child category relationships with a maximum of **2 nesting levels** (parent and child):

- **Food** (parent)
  - Groceries (child)
  - Restaurants (child)
  - Coffee (child)
- **Transport** (parent)
  - Public transport (child)
  - Taxi (child)
  - Fuel (child)

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info"}
Only leaf categories (categories without children) can have transactions assigned to them. Parent categories aggregate statistics from their children.
::

## Creating a Category

Go to **Categories** → **+**. Fill in:

- **Name** - required, must be unique within the same parent
- **Parent category** - optional, pick a root category or **Without parent**. When a parent is selected, the color is copied from it
- **Child categories** - for root categories only: pick existing root categories or children of other parents to move them under this one. Removing two or more children at once asks for confirmation; they become root categories
- **Icon** - pick from the [Material Design Icons](https://icones.js.org/collection/mdi) gallery or type any icon name. Default: random icon
- **Color** - palette picker with a live preview. Default: random color

Default settings: **Recent** is on, **Favorite** is off.

### Optional Settings

For categories **without children**:

| Setting | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Favorite category | Shows in the **Favorites** section of the [category selector](/en/guide/transactions#how-quick-category-selection-works) |
| Recent category | Allowed in the **Recent** section (max 16, sorted by most recent transaction date, no overlap with favorites) |
| Exclude from statistics | Transactions of this category are left out of the dashboard totals and charts unless the category itself is selected |

For categories **with children**:

| Setting | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Apply color to all child categories | Updates every child's color to match the parent |

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info"}
A category cannot become a parent if it already has transactions assigned to it. Move or delete those transactions first.
::

## Category List

- **Toggle folders** (top-right) expands or collapses all parent categories at once; clicking a parent row expands only that one.
- **View settings** (sliders icon): choose the row **Background** (none, standard, category color), how children are shown (**List** or **Cloud** of round icons), and whether to **Show children count** next to parents.
- The **⋮** menu on a row (or right-click / long-press) offers **Open**, **Edit**, **Add to favorites** / **Show in recent** toggles, and **Delete**.

## Deleting

**Deleting a category** removes all its transactions - the confirmation dialog shows how many. Categories with children cannot be deleted - delete or move the children first.

## Special Categories

Finapp uses two special category identifiers that are always present and cannot be edited or deleted:

- **Transfer** (`transfer`) - automatically assigned to transfer transactions. Cannot be used for regular transactions. See [Transfers](/en/guide/transfers).
- **Adjustment** (`adjustment`) - used for balance adjustments. Adjustment transactions are excluded from regular statistics. See [Transactions](/en/guide/transactions#adjustments) for more details.

## Category Page

Click a category to open its page: the same statistics layout as the [Dashboard](/en/guide/statistics), filtered to that category. If the category only has income (or only expenses), the layout collapses to that single type.

For parent categories, a **Child categories** block lists the children - click one to open its page. The block can be hidden in the view settings.

Actions available from the **⋮** menu: **Edit** and **Delete**.

## Statistics by Category

The dashboard breaks spending down by category - as a list, a cloud of icons, or comparison bars - and lets you click any category to focus the whole page on it. See [Statistics](/en/guide/statistics#blocks).
