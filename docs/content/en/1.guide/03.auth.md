## Google Sign-In

**Sign in with Google** is the only way to create an account. Sign in with your Google account to:

- Sync data across all your devices
- Keep your financial data backed up on the server
- Access your data from any browser

### How to Sign In

1. Open [finapp.ilko.me](https://finapp.ilko.me) or launch the installed app
2. Tap **Sign in with Google**
3. Select your Google account and authorize
4. Finapp loads your wallets, categories, and transactions

On first sign-in, Finapp walks you through an onboarding flow: create your first wallet and category to get started.

## Demo Mode

Don't want to create an account? Use demo mode to explore Finapp with pre-generated data:

- About 900 randomly generated transactions over the last 2 years (salary, seasonal spending, transfers, a few adjustments)
- 32 categories with a parent-child hierarchy
- 8 wallets in different currencies (including a credit card and an archived wallet)
- All features work - create, edit, delete, statistics

Data is stored locally in your browser - nothing is sent to the server.

See [Settings](/en/guide/settings#demo-mode) for how to enable, update, or exit demo mode.

## Cross-Device Sync

Once signed in, your data syncs automatically across all devices logged in with the same Google account. Changes appear in real time - add a transaction on your phone and it shows up on your desktop within seconds.

Your **language**, **base currency** and **saved dashboard views** are also synced to the server, so they are restored on every device you log into.

## Offline Mode

Finapp works without an internet connection. Here's how it works:

### What happens when you go offline

- The app continues to work - data is read from the local database on your device
- You can create, edit, and delete transactions, wallets, and categories
- Every change is saved locally and added to an upload queue

### What happens when you reconnect

- Queued changes are uploaded to the server automatically, in the order you made them
- If you created a wallet offline and then added a transaction to it, the relationships are preserved
- Changes made on other devices while you were offline are downloaded

The account menu at the bottom of the sidebar shows a dot while you are offline or have unsynced changes; **Settings** → **Account** shows how many changes are still waiting.

### Limitations

- Exchange rates don't update without internet - the last downloaded rates are used
- Cross-device sync only resumes once you're back online
- You cannot sign out while unsynced changes remain - connect to the internet first, so nothing is lost

## Sign Out

Go to **Settings** → **Account** → **Logout**, or use the account menu. Signing out clears all local data from the device. When you sign back in, everything syncs back from the server.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-alert-triangle" color="warning"}
Signing out on one device does not affect your data on other devices - everything remains safely stored on the server.
::

## Deleting Your Data

To permanently delete all your data, go to **Settings** → **Delete my data**. This removes all wallets, categories, transactions and saved views from the server. The action requires confirmation and cannot be undone. Your Google account stays linked - sign in again to start from scratch.

See [Settings](/en/guide/settings#data-deletion) for details.
