## Overview

The **Dashboard** is a stack of blocks - period selection, totals, chart, category breakdowns and transactions - that you can rearrange, hide and configure. [Adjustment](/en/guide/transactions#adjustments) transactions and categories marked **Exclude from statistics** are left out of all totals.

Wallet and category pages use the same layout, filtered to that wallet or category.

## Period Selection

The row under the title controls which dates you are looking at:

- **Previous / next** arrows step one period back or forward. They can be hidden in the view settings.
- The **period button** (e.g. *Last 14 days*) opens a picker with three tabs:
  - **Presets** - today, this week, this month, this year, last 7/14/30 days, last 3/6/12 months, last 6 years, and two "everything" presets: **To today** (from your first transaction until today) and **All** (only periods that have transactions).
  - **Calendar** - pick a custom start and end date.
  - **Grouped by** - the interval the chart is split into: day, week, month or year.
- **Quick date selection** - an optional row of preset chips under the chart. Enable it in the view settings and choose which presets to show.

The chart can also be **dragged** left or right to pan the period without opening the picker, and the granularity selector above the chart (*Day*, *Week*, ...) regroups the intervals in place.

## Filtering

The **All wallets and categories** button opens the filter: pick any number of wallets and categories. Selected ones are shown as chips in the button label, and the filter is kept between sessions.

### Shareable URLs

Filter and period settings live in the URL (`?filterWallets=...&filterCategories=...` plus the date parameters). Copy the URL to share or bookmark a specific view.

## Blocks

Every block has a switch in the view settings and can be reordered by dragging. In order of the default layout:

### Spending and income

Three cards - **Spending**, **Income** and **Net** - with a mini pie of the category shares. Click a card to focus the whole page on that type (spending-only or income-only); click again to go back. The block can be pinned so it stays visible while you scroll.

### Wallets

Off by default. Shows the wallets used in the period with either their current **balance** or the amount moved **for the period**. Clicking a wallet filters the page to it - in single or multiple selection mode. Configure the count, icons and display mode in the view settings.

### Main chart

A bar, line or pie chart of the selected period, split by the chosen interval. The sliders button in the chart corner opens the chart toolbar:

- **Chart style** - bars, lines or pie. Lines have their own options: points, smoothing, gradient, skip zero values.
- **Chart data** - **Income and spending** (one series per type) or **By categories** (one series per category, grouped by parent or by child).
- **Chart layout** - income and spending together (wide or narrow) or as two separate charts.
- **Page layout** - **Combined** (one column) or **Split** (spending on the left, income on the right). Desktop only.
- **Bar layout** - side by side or stacked; **Value direction** - all bars upward or signed.
- **Pie** - donut or filled circle, labels, percentages.

Further switches in the view settings: **Show average line** (mean of the visible intervals), **Show background**, **Show scale and grid lines**.

Click a bar or point to select that interval - the blocks below switch to it. Click again to return to the whole period.

### Comparison bars

Off by default. One bar per category, height proportional to the amount, with the icon underneath. Tooltips can list child categories.

### Category cloud

Round category icons with the amount. Options: **Name and amount in one row**, **Hide other categories when selected**, show **favorites** and **recent** categories even when they have no transactions.

### Categories list

One row per category with the amount and a **mini chart** of that category over the intervals (bars, bars with a plus, lines, or hidden). Options: **Background** (none, standard, category color), **Amount lines**, **Icons background**, **Automatically expand parent categories**.

Each category block has its own **Grouping**: **Automatic**, **By parent** (children roll up into parents, expandable) or **By child category**.

### Click to focus

Click any category in a breakdown to focus the entire page - chart, lists and transactions - on it. Click again to clear. Long-press a category to create a transaction in it.

### Transactions

The list of transactions in the period, grouped by day. Options: **Title**, **Show transaction type tabs** (All / Expense / Income / Transfers), **Show previous transactions** (rows from before the period, loaded on scroll).

### Average totals

Off by default. Shows the historical average per period over the last N periods (e.g. average monthly spending over the last 10 months). Set the count in the view settings.

## View Settings

The sliders icon in the header opens the **View Settings** panel (desktop: a side panel, mobile: a bottom sheet). At the top: the saved views bar and the **Page layout** select; then the blocks, either listed as **Blocks** (drag to reorder, switch on and off, open to configure) or as **Rules**.

Each block panel has an **Apply to all views** button that copies its settings to every saved view.

## Saved Views

A view is the whole dashboard configuration - blocks, their order and settings. Views are synced to your account, so they follow you between devices.

- **Save as new** / **Update current** - keep the current configuration under a name (a name is generated if you leave it empty).
- The **Views** select at the top of the panel switches between saved views.
- **Activate automatically** - give a view a condition and it becomes active whenever the condition holds. Conditions can be combined with AND / OR and compare the **period** (e.g. longer than 3 months), the **content width** in pixels (a compact layout for narrow windows), the **number of categories**, or the **wallet / category** you are looking at (any wallet page, a specific category, ...).
- The **⋯** menu next to it renames, sorts (drag-and-drop) or deletes views.

### Block rules

Inside a view, a single block can have rules of its own: *when the period is longer than a year, hide the transactions*; *when the content is narrower than 600 px, switch the chart to a pie*. Add a rule under **Configure with rules**, pick the conditions and the parameters it overrides. The **Rules** tab of the view settings lists every rule across blocks.
