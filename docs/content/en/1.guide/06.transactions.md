## Overview

Transactions are the core of Finapp. Every financial operation - a purchase, salary, or transfer between accounts - is recorded as a transaction.

---

---
::

## Transaction Types

| Type | What it does | When to use |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Expense** | Decreases wallet balance | Daily spending, bills, subscriptions |
| **Income** | Increases wallet balance | Salary, freelance, gifts |
| **Transfer** | Moves money between wallets | ATM withdrawal, currency exchange, moving savings |
| **Adjustment** | Corrects wallet balance | Found discrepancy, lost cash, initial balance |

## Creating a Transaction

**Desktop:** click **Add transaction** in the sidebar or the floating button in the bottom-right corner to open the form in a side panel. Keyboard shortcut: `Cmd+G`. The panel has two slides: the form (with favorite and recent categories below the calculator) and a **History** slide with filter tabs (all transactions / by wallet / by wallet + category).

**Mobile:** tap the **+** button in the bottom bar. The form opens as a bottom sheet with three slides - swipe between them:

1. **History** - recent transactions filtered by the current wallet and category. Tap to edit.
2. **Form** (default) - date, type toggle, amount with calculator, wallet and category selectors, description.
3. **Quick selector** - a wallets grid and the favorite / recent categories. Tap to select and return to the form.

The form needs at least one wallet and one category - until then, the onboarding screen guides you to create them.

#### How quick category selection works

Categories on the quick selector are displayed in priority order:

1. **Favorites** - categories with the "Favorite" setting enabled. Shown first in a separate section. Configured manually in each category's settings.
2. **Recent** - categories from your latest transactions (up to 16). Sorted by most recent usage date. Don't overlap with favorites - if a category is already in favorites, it won't appear in recent.
3. **All categories** - if there are no favorites and no recent categories, all available categories are displayed.

You can control visibility in each category's settings: **Favorite** (show in quick selector) and **Recent** (show in the recent list).

### Type Toggle

Switch between **Spending**, **Income**, and **Transfer** at the top of the form. The Transfer tab only appears when 2+ wallets exist.

### Calculator

The amount field has a built-in calculator with buttons for `+`, `−`, `×`, `÷` and decimal point. Enter an expression like `150+50` - the submit button changes to `=` to confirm the calculation. After pressing `=`, it shows the result and the submit button becomes a checkmark.

Long-press the **C** (clear) button to reset all amount fields.

### Category Selector

Tap the category to open the selector: a searchable tree of all categories with the **Adjustment** entry among the roots. You can switch between a list and a grid, filter to favorites, expand or collapse parents, and create or edit a category right there without leaving the form.

### Wallet Selector

Shows the current wallet with icon, name, currency, and balance. Tap to open the wallet list.

### Date Picker

Shows the selected date with day, month, and weekday. Navigation buttons: previous day, next day. Tap the date to open a calendar. Future dates are not allowed.

### Description

Tap the message icon next to the date to add an optional text note. A dot indicator appears on the icon when a description is set.

### Submit Button

- **Checkmark** - ready to save (amount > 0)
- **Equals** - calculator expression entered, press to calculate first
- **Disabled** - amount is 0

### Validation

The form checks before saving:

- Amount must be greater than 0
- Wallet must be selected
- Category must be selected (except for transfers)
- For transfers: expense and income wallets must be different

## Adjustments

Balance corrections that don't count as income or expense. Use when your actual wallet balance differs from what Finapp shows.

- **Income + Adjustment** - increase balance
- **Expense + Adjustment** - decrease balance

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info"}
Adjustment transactions are excluded from statistics calculations but included in wallet balance.
::

### When to use adjustments

**Initial balance.** You created a "Cash" wallet, but you already have $500 on hand. Create an income adjustment for $500 - the wallet balance becomes correct without showing a false income in statistics.

**Discrepancy with reality.** Finapp shows $320 in cash, but you actually have $300. Create an expense adjustment for $20 - the balance evens out without distorting expense statistics.

**Cashback or bonuses.** You received $15 cashback on your card. It's not income in the usual sense, but the balance changed. An income adjustment reflects the balance change without inflating income statistics.

## Editing, Duplicating and Deleting

Click any transaction in a list to open it in the form. Edit and save, or delete with the trash icon - a confirmation dialog prevents accidental deletion.

Every transaction row also has a menu - right-click on desktop, long-press on mobile, or the **⋯** button in History - with **Filter by date** (opens the dashboard on that day), **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Select** and **Delete**. **Duplicate** opens the form pre-filled with the original's amount, category, wallet, date and description, ready to save as a new record. Useful for recurring expenses.

### Bulk Editing

**Select** starts a selection; pick more rows and a bar appears at the top of the page. From it you can change the **category**, **wallet**, **date** or **description** of every selected transaction at once, or delete them. Transfers keep their wallets when you bulk-change the wallet.

## Search

Press `Cmd+K` or use **Search** in the sidebar to find categories, wallets and transactions by name or description. Pick a result to open it; transactions also offer **Edit**, **Duplicate** and **Delete** right from the results.

## History Page

The History page (`/history`) is a table of every transaction. Above it:

- **Search** - matches the description, category and wallet names.
- **All types** - filter to Expense, Income, Transfer or Adjustment.
- **All descriptions** - only transactions with, or without, a description.
- **All wallets and categories** - the same wallet/category filter as the dashboard.
- **Period** - start and end date.
- **Amount** - minimum and maximum.

Every filter is stored in the URL, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view; the reset button clears them all. The count of matching transactions is shown above the table.

The table scrolls virtually, so even thousands of rows stay fast. Click a column header to sort, and open the **Columns** menu to hide columns, move them left or right, pin them to the left, or reset the layout. Column widths are draggable.

The checkbox on the left selects rows for [bulk editing](#bulk-editing); the header checkbox selects every row that matches the filters.
