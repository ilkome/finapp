## Overview

Wallets represent your financial accounts - bank accounts, cash, cards, or any other place where you keep money.

## Wallet Types

Each wallet has a type that determines its behavior:

- **Cash** - physical cash
- **Cashless** - bank accounts and digital payment systems
- **Credit** - credit cards (supports credit limit)
- **Deposit** - savings deposits
- **Crypto** - cryptocurrency wallets
- **Debt** - loans and debts

## Creating a Wallet

Go to **Wallets** → **+**. Fill in:

- **Name** - required, displayed everywhere
- **Currency** - opens a searchable modal with 180+ fiat and crypto currencies. Currencies you already use appear first. Search by code or name. Default: **USD**
- **Color** - opens a palette with a custom color input and a live preview of the wallet icon
- **Description** - optional notes, shown on the wallet page
- **Type** - cash (default), cashless, credit, crypto, deposit, debt

### Optional Settings

| Setting | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Credit limit | Only shown for **credit** type. The wallet page and statistics show debt, available credit, and the limit |
| Withdrawal | Marks the wallet as money you can actually withdraw. Feeds the **Withdrawal** and **Left after credit cards** statistics |
| Excluded from totals | Wallet balance is not counted in **Total** or in the per-type rows |
| Archived | Hidden from the list and excluded from all statistics rows unless you turn the matching option on |

## Wallet List

### Grouping

The tabs above the list switch between a flat **List**, grouping by **Type**, and grouping by **Currencies**. When grouped, two buttons appear on the right:

- **Toggle grouping** - nest the other grouping inside (currency → type or type → currency). Each group and sub-group can be collapsed independently.
- **Toggle folders** - expand or collapse all groups at once.

Group headers show the group total in your base currency (plus the native total for currency groups). Turn on **Show group counts** in the menu to see how many wallets each group holds.

### Currency Filter

When you have wallets in more than one currency, a strip of currency chips (All / RUB / USD / ...) sits above the statistics. Pick one to limit both the list and the statistics to that currency. The strip is hidden while grouping by currency.

### Statistics

The statistics block shows totals in your base currency, one row per slice:

| Row | Shows |
|-----|-------|
| Total | Every wallet except credit cards and the ones kept out of the total |
| Withdrawal | Wallets with the **Withdrawal** option enabled |
| Left after credit cards | Withdrawal total minus what is owed on credit cards. Only shown when both exist |
| Not included in total | Wallets marked **Excluded from totals** |
| Archived | Archived wallets |
| Cash / Cashless / Deposit / Credit / Crypto / Debt | Total per wallet type. Credit also shows the used and total limit |

Rows with a zero amount are hidden. Click a row to filter the list to that slice; click **Total** to go back. If a filter becomes empty (for example after switching currency), the list falls back to **Total** automatically.

Two rows - **Total** and **Left after credit cards** - are **pinned** by default and rendered as large cards above the list. Open **⋮ → Statistics** to:

- pin or unpin rows and drag them to reorder the pinned cards,
- hide rows from the list and reorder the remaining ones,
- read what each row means (the **i** button).

Pinned, hidden and ordered rows are remembered separately for each grouping mode.

### Menu

The **⋮** button (shown when you have more than one wallet) opens:

- **Sort wallets** - drag-and-drop reordering. The order is synced with your other devices and used everywhere: the sidebar, selectors, and the dashboard.
- **Statistics** - the rows panel described above.
- **Show archived wallets** - include archived wallets in the list.
- **Include archived in statistics** and **Include not-in-total in statistics** - count those wallets in the per-type rows and the total.
- **Show group counts** - number of wallets next to each group header.

## Wallet Page

Click a wallet to open its page. It shows the balance (for credit wallets with a limit: **Debt**, **Available** and **Limit**), the description, and the same statistics layout as the [Dashboard](/en/guide/statistics) filtered to that wallet. The **Wallet balance** and **Wallet description** blocks can be hidden in the view settings.

From the **⋮** menu: **Edit** opens the wallet form, **Delete** removes the wallet and all its transactions. The confirmation dialog shows how many transactions will be affected.

The sidebar on desktop lists all wallets with their balances - click one to open its page; right-click for **Open**, **Edit** and **Delete**.

## Initial Balance

New wallets start with a zero balance. To set the initial balance, create an **Adjustment** transaction with the desired amount. Adjustments don't affect statistics, so your reports stay clean. See [Transactions - Adjustments](/en/guide/transactions#adjustments) for details.

## Balance Calculation

Wallet balance is calculated as the sum of all transactions: income adds to the balance, expenses subtract from it. Transfers between wallets adjust both wallet balances accordingly.

## Multi-Currency

Each wallet has its own currency. Finapp fetches exchange rates daily and converts all balances to your base currency for unified totals. See [Settings - Exchange Rates](/en/guide/settings#exchange-rates).
