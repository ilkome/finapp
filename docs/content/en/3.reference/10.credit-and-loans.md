## Overview

Two sub-domains share the `credit` wallet type but answer different questions:

- **Credit product** - any wallet with `type: 'credit'`. Covers revolving debt (credit cards, BNPL) that has no fixed schedule. Tracked through its own transactions (payments in, interest and fees as its expenses), the minimum-payment fields bank sync pushes onto the wallet, and "due this month".
- **Loan** - a credit wallet with a `loans` row on top: an amortized contract with a generated payment schedule, optional overrides, and the reconciliation/summary engine in `app/app/components/loans/engine`.

Every loan is a credit product; not every credit product is a loan (a plain credit card has no `loans` row and reports only its bank-synced minimum payment).

## Entities

### `loans` (Supabase table + PowerSync)

One row per loan contract, keyed by wallet. Fields (`app/app/components/loans/types.ts`, `loanItemSchema`):

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| `walletId` | The credit wallet this contract belongs to |
| `principalAmount`, `annualRate`, `termMonths` | Contract terms. `annualRate` is `null` when the bank publishes no contract rate (`0` is a real interest-free loan); the engine then projects generated rows, planned interest and what-if at the rate the paid interest (or the bank schedule) implies, and hides what-if when there is neither |
| `startDate`, `firstPaymentDate` | Civil days (UTC-midnight ms-epoch); `firstPaymentDate` is stated by the contract, never derived from `startDate` |
| `paymentDay` | 1-31, clamped to the length of each month |
| `scheduleType` | `'annuity'` or `'differentiated'` |
| `overpaymentMode` | `'reducePayment'` or `'reduceTerm'` - how the unpaid tail reacts to a prepayment |
| `interestMethod` | `'monthly'` (rate / 12) or `'daily'` (actual/365, like most banks) |
| `lateAfterDays` | Days after the due date a payment still counts as on time (default 3) |
| `prepayWindowDays` | A payment more than this many days before the next due date is a prepayment (default 15) |
| `debitWalletId` | Wallet the payment is debited from (optional, enables a "not enough money" signal) |
| `contractNumber`, `desc` | Free text |
| `bankDebtAmount`, `bankDebtUpdatedAt` | Debt the bank reports, pushed by bank sync; the wallet is checked against it (`bankDebtCheck`) |
| `nextPaymentDate`, `nextPaymentPrincipal`, `nextPaymentInterest` | The bank's own split of the next payment, pushed by bank sync |
| `updatedAt` | ms-epoch |

### `loan_schedule_rows` (Supabase table + PowerSync)

Only rows that differ from the generated schedule are stored - an untouched payment is represented by its absence. Fields (`loanScheduleRowSchema`): `loanId`, `paymentNumber`, `date`, `totalAmount`, `principalPart`, `interestPart`, `source` (`'bank' | 'manual'`), `updatedAt`.

### New columns on existing tables

- `wallets.minPaymentAmount`, `wallets.minPaymentDate`, `wallets.minPaymentUpdatedAt` - minimum-payment summary pushed by bank sync for revolving credit wallets.

### Deterministic ids

Ids are client-generated, not database-assigned, so two offline devices editing the same loan converge on the same row instead of hitting a unique-constraint conflict:

- `loanIdFor(walletId)` -> `loan:<walletId>`
- `loanScheduleRowIdFor(loanId, paymentNumber)` -> `lsr:<loanId>:<n>`

Same scheme as the bank-sync `loans-import` script, so an imported loan and a manually-created one for the same wallet land on the same row.

### No foreign keys

`app/supabase/migrations/20260923120000_loans.sql` declares no foreign-key constraints between `loans`, `loan_schedule_rows`, `trns` and `wallets`. PowerSync does not guarantee upload order between tables, so a constraint would fail on a legitimate offline write ordering. Uniqueness instead comes from deterministic ids plus `unique ("userId", "walletId")` on `loans` and `unique ("loanId", "paymentNumber")` on `loan_schedule_rows`.

## Invariants

- A payment is **one transfer into** the credit wallet for the whole amount, from any wallet. A transfer out of a credit wallet is spending on the product; only the income side is collected into the loan ledger (`collectCreditTrns` in `app/app/components/loans/engine/ledger.ts`).
- **Interest and fees** are what the bank takes out of the payment: ordinary Expense transactions **of the credit wallet itself** with `categoryId: 'loanInterest'` or `categoryId: 'loanFine'`. The principal repaid is payment - interest - fees, so the credit wallet balance stays the negative principal left.
- Example: a 12 500 payment with 9 800 interest is `Debit card -> Car loan` 12 500 plus a `loanInterest` expense of 9 800 on `Car loan`. Transfer exactly what the bank debits: money sent more than `prepayWindowDays` ahead of the due date reads as a prepayment. Amounts are kept to the kopeck.
- `loanInterest` and `loanFine` are **reserved** categories (`isReservedCategoryId` in `app/app/components/categories/utils.ts`): synthetic, never editable, deletable or reparented, like `transfer` and `adjustment`. Unlike `transfer` and `adjustment` they are **not system categories** - they are ordinary expenses and **do count** in income/expense statistics.
- A credit wallet's balance is negative debt. Anything the loan engine calls "remaining" reads `-(walletBalance)`.

## Engine pipeline

Pure functions in `app/app/components/loans/engine`, composed in `derive.ts`:

1. **`generateSchedule(params, overrides)`** (`schedule.ts`) - builds the row-by-row plan (annuity or differentiated), splicing in `overrides` (rows sourced from a bank statement or entered by hand) in place of the generated ones. A holiday row (nothing due, `totalAmount <= 0 && principalPart <= 0`) pushes the term one month further out instead of shrinking it. Interest is `rate / 12` a month (`monthly`), except a first period that is not exactly a month from `startDate`, which accrues daily; `daily` accrues actual/365 over every period. The annuity payment is solved over the whole schedule (overrides, holidays, short periods included), so the last row never absorbs a gap.
2. **`reconcileSchedule(params, rows, trns, today)`** (`reconcile.ts`) - matches real transactions onto schedule rows sequentially (transactions by date, rows by number), to the kopeck. A `payment` adds to the row's principal, `interest` and `fine` add to their part and are taken out of its principal, whichever lands first. Interest or a fine on the day of the row's payment always stays in that row. A row is covered when its principal OR its whole amount is paid: a bank re-accrues interest by days, so on a bank row the principal matches and the total moves, on a generated row it is the other way round. Bank and manual rows must match exactly; a generated row is our estimate and allows 1% short. Every transaction lands in exactly one row. When the cursor leaves a row, what it got beyond its total (only when the principal was overpaid too, so a payment without its interest or a little extra interest is not a prepayment) first pays the following rows already due on that day (a skipped month caught up, with no transactions of its own). The balance then follows the principal actually repaid (a payment without its interest is assumed to have paid the planned interest), and any deviation re-solves the unpaid tail through `rebuildTail` right away, so kopecks carry into the tail and later payments are matched against the plan in force. The one date window: a payment more than `prepayWindowDays` before the next due date, with the previous row closed, is a prepayment on its own day and never opens a row; the interest or fine charged with it that day goes with it. Produces a `status` per row: `scheduled`, `paid`, `partial`, `overdue`, or `late` (paid more than `lateAfterDays` after the due date).
3. **`summarizeLoan(params, rows, walletBalance)`** (`summary.ts`) - totals paid/planned principal, interest and fines; computes `effectiveRate` (paid interest annualized over the average real opening balance it was charged on - answers "what did this loan actually cost", not a restatement of the contract rate) `unrecognized` (the gap between the wallet balance and the reconciled principal; 0 when everything matches), `interestMissing` (settled payments recorded without their interest), `projectionRate`, and `nextPayment` as what is still owed on the first unpaid row.
4. **`whatIf(params, rows, extra, mode, today)`** (`summary.ts`) - interest saved and new end date if `extra` went to the principal right now, starting from the real debt and re-solving the unpaid tail as if it were freshly generated.
5. **`derivePortfolio(input)`** (`portfolio.ts`) - everything the loans page needs across all loans in one pass, converted into the base currency: totals, an avalanche pick (largest interest saved) and a snowball pick (smallest remaining balance) for one extra payment.

`rebuildTail` (in `schedule.ts`) is the shared re-solver behind both overpayment reconciliation and `whatIf`: `reducePayment` re-solves the payment over the same row count, `reduceTerm` keeps the payment and runs out of balance earlier, dropping rows that are no longer needed. Bank and manual rows are never recalculated, only re-chained onto the running balance; `reducePayment` solves the payment over the whole tail, so bank rows in it keep their own principal instead of the last generated row absorbing it.

`ledger.ts` also exposes `costOf(trns)`, which totals interest and fees for one credit wallet and buckets them by civil month - used for both loans and plain revolving wallets.

## Where things live

- **Pure engine**: `app/app/components/loans/engine/{types,schedule,reconcile,summary,ledger,derive,portfolio}.ts`. No Vue, no store, no I/O.
- **Store**: `app/app/components/loans/useLoansStore.ts`. Holds `items` (loans) and `scheduleRows` state, watches the `loans` and `loan_schedule_rows` PowerSync tables, and exposes computeds (`byWalletId`, `costByWalletId`, `dueThisMonth`, `trnsByCreditWallet`) that call the pure engine functions with live store data. CRUD (`saveLoan`, `deleteLoan`, `saveScheduleRow`, `deleteScheduleRow`) follows the same optimistic-update-then-`upsertRow`/`deleteRow` pattern as the other stores.
- **Views vs containers**: per `AGENTS.md`, `*View.vue` components under `app/app/components/loans/` (e.g. `ScheduleView`, `SummaryView`, `CostView`, `PortfolioView`, `RecommendationView`, `RevolvingView`, `CardLineView`, `WhatIfView`) are pure - props in, emits out, JSON-serializable props. `Page.vue` is the container: it owns `useLoansStore`, `useWalletsStore`, `useCurrenciesStore`, converts between currencies, and passes plain values down to the views.
- **Minimum payment**: `app/app/components/loans/minPayment.ts` derives a revolving wallet's due/overdue/paid/stale status from `wallets.minPaymentAmount/minPaymentDate/minPaymentUpdatedAt` and the payments into the wallet seen since the bank last refreshed the summary.
- **Wallet page figures**: `principalFree.ts` flags calendar months with no principal repaid (interest-only or a holiday row); `bankDebt.ts` compares the wallet with `loans.bankDebtAmount` and goes stale once a payment fell due after the bank figure. `presenters.ts` maps both, plus the overpayment (paid interest and fines as a share of principal) and an effective rate shown only when it differs from the contract rate by more than 0.5 pp, into `SummaryView`/`ScheduleView` props.
- **"Due this month"**: `deriveDue` in `derive.ts`, called from the store's `dueThisMonth` computed. Merges every overdue or partial row of each loan (what is still owed) plus its next scheduled payment with each revolving wallet's minimum payment over a rolling 31-day window, converted to the base currency.

## Testing

- Unit tests per engine module: `app/app/components/loans/engine/{schedule,reconcile,derive,ledger,portfolio,banks,principalFree,bankDebt}.test.ts`.
- Fixtures under `app/app/components/demo/loans/`: `loan-{a,b,c}.json` are anonymized bank schedules (amounts and rates rescaled, dates shifted) that keep the banks' real behavior (daily interest, holidays, early payoff); the rest feed the view tests.
- View tests: one `*.view.test.ts` per pure view (`CardLineView`, `WhatIfView`, `RevolvingView`, `ScheduleView`, `CostView`, `PortfolioView`, `SummaryView`, `RecommendationView`), mounted from fixtures per the `View` testing rule in `AGENTS.md`.
- Store and helper tests: `useLoansStore.test.ts`, `walletBalanceItems.test.ts`, `minPayment.test.ts`, `types.test.ts`.
